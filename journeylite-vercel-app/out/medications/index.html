GLP-1 Receptor Agonist

WeGovy® (Semaglutide)

WeGovy contains semaglutide, the same active ingredient in Ozempic®, but at a higher dose specifically approved for weight management. It works by mimicking a gut hormone that reduces appetite and slows digestion.

AdministrationWeekly injection
Avg. Weight Loss~15% body weight on average
StatusFDA-approved for chronic weight management

GLP-1 / GIP Dual Agonist

Zepbound® (Tirzepatide)

Zepbound (tirzepatide) is the same medication as Mounjaro® at FDA-approved weight loss doses. As a dual GLP-1/GIP agonist, it offers slightly greater average weight loss than semaglutide-based medications.

AdministrationWeekly injection
Avg. Weight Loss~20% body weight on average
StatusFDA-approved for chronic weight management

Appetite Suppressant

Adipex® (Phentermine)

Phentermine is one of the most widely prescribed weight loss medications, suppressing appetite through the central nervous system. It's typically used short-term alongside lifestyle modifications.

AdministrationDaily oral tablet
Avg. Weight LossShort-term aid; varies
StatusFDA-approved for short-term weight management
