This list is meant to be a resource, **but keep in mind that not every meal on each site is specifically recommended by our dietitians**. If you have a question about specific meals, we encourage you to speak with your dietitian to make sure you are making the most informed choices possible.

-   **Apps/Websites:** 
    -   [**MealLime**](https://www.mealime.com/): Weekly recipes, automated shopping lists, delicious meals in 30 minutes or less. 
    -   [**EMeals**](https://emeals.com/?gclid=CjwKCAiAxJSPBhAoEiwAeO_fP_Qa2IISxSbN3OIOiMuY9Jx3xcCy2_GzjxABYry2XaX-RJU5JLPqLRoCPooQAvD_BwE): Weekly recipes, automated shopping lists with delivery options, 15 different meal plan styles to choose from. 
    -   [**PlateJoy**](https://www.platejoy.com/): Nutritionist designed and supported, free with some health insurance providers, generates shopping lists. 
    -   [**Meal Prep Pro**](https://mealpreppro.com/): Easy recipes for daily or weekly cooking, nutritional information on all recipes, personalized recipes, syncs with fitness devices, generates smart shopping lists. 
    -   [**Meal Board**](https://www.mealboard.com/): Digital grocery list with manually added prices to stay in-budget for the week, personalized pantry is edited when you use any of your ingredients. 
    -   [**Eat This Much**](https://www.eatthismuch.com/): Calorie goal oriented, nutritional info per recipe, large database of restaurants and packaged foods, barcode scanner for nutritional information. 
    -   [**The Real Food Dietitians**](https://therealfooddietitians.com/): Recipes, free meal plans, mostly gluten free and designed to be customizable for special diets and allergies.
    -   [**Downshiftology**](https://downshiftology.com/)**:** Free weekly recipes, kitchen advice and cooking tutorials.
    -   [**Skinnytaste**](https://www.skinnytaste.com/): Meal plans and recipes. Includes nutritional information on all recipes. Recipes range from high protein, macro -friendly, high-fiber, gluten-free, anti-inflammatory!
