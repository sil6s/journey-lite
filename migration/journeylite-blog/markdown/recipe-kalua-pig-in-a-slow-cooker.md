“Slow-cooker pork butt roast with delicious smoky taste.”

INGREDIENTS:

<table border="0" width="100%" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0"><tbody><tr><td valign="top" width="50%">1 (6 pound) pork butt roast1 1/2 tablespoons Hawaiian sea salt</td><td valign="top" width="50%">1 tablespoon liquid smoke flavoring</td></tr></tbody></table>

DIRECTIONS:

<table border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0"><tbody><tr><td valign="top">1.</td><td valign="top">Pierce pork all over with a carving fork. Rub salt then liquid smoke over meat. Place roast in a slow cooker.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">2.</td><td valign="top">Cover, and cook on Low for 16 to 20 hours, turning once during cooking time.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">3.</td><td valign="top">Remove meat from slow cooker, and shred, adding drippings as needed to moisten.</td></tr></tbody></table>
