**MEAT AND MEAT SUBSTITUTES**

Meat and meat substitutes that contain both protein and fat are on this list. In general, one meat exchange is:

-   1oz of meat, fish, poultry, or cheese
-   ½ cup of beans, peas, or lentils

Based on the amount of fat they contain meats are divided into four categories:

-   Very lean
-   Lean
-   Medium-fat
-   High-fat
