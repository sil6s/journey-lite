## What Is Gastric Sleeve Surgery?

Gastric sleeve surgery reduces the size of the stomach into a smaller sleeve-shaped pouch. This helps patients feel full sooner, eat smaller portions, and often experience changes in hunger signaling.

Unlike gastric bypass or SADI/SIPS, the sleeve does not involve rerouting the intestines. For many patients, this makes it a strong balance of effectiveness, simplicity, and long-term durability.

### Potential Benefits

-   Powerful weight loss for many patients
-   No intestinal bypass
-   May improve diabetes, sleep apnea, blood pressure, and joint pain
-   Often more durable than diet or medication alone
-   Minimally invasive approach in appropriate patients

### Important Considerations

-   It is still surgery and requires medical evaluation
-   Patients must commit to nutrition and lifestyle changes
-   Protein intake, hydration, and vitamins matter long term
-   Individual results and risks vary

Is gastric sleeve better than GLP-1 medication?

It depends on the patient. GLP-1 medications can be helpful, but they often require ongoing use and ongoing monthly cost. Gastric sleeve surgery is a one-time procedure designed to create more durable physiologic changes. A consultation can help compare your options.

Candidacy depends on BMI, medical history, prior surgery history, medications, anesthesia risk, and surgeon evaluation. Patients with significant medical issues may require hospital-based surgery and may not qualify for this self-pay surgery center offer.
