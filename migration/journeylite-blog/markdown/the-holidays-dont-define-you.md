What you did or didn’t do, what you ate or didn’t eat, doesn’t make you “good” or “bad” – it makes you human.
Eating off-plan or moving less than usual isn’t a failure. Success isn’t about perfection; it’s about how gently and confidently you return to your routines.

If getting back on track feels hard, take a moment this week to write down the (https://journeylite.com/reset-diet-or-habit-refresh-why-quick-fixes-arent-the-answer-to-long-term-bariatric-success/) before the holidays. Start there. Restart with intention, not guilt. You’ve done it before, and you can absolutely do it again.
