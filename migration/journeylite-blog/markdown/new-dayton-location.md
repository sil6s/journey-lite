2621 Dryden Road, Morraine, OH 45439

We are very pleased to announce that construction has started on our Dayton location at 2621 Dryden Road in Morraine, just south of Dayton! Those who have been to our Elizabeth Place location know that it is just a time share, but this will not be the case in the new location. This will be a full-time office for us, staffed with a provider and a registered dietitian 3 days a week. If you are familiar with the Dryden Road exit off of I-75, the northbound exit ramp actually wraps around the building property, which you can easily see from the highway. This will be much more convenient for our patients as the sprawling Elizabeth Place complex requires quite a bit of walking once you park your car.

Stay tuned for more info, but we are targeting an opening date around August 1st!
