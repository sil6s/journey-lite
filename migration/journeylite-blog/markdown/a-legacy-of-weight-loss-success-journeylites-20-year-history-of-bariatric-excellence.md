For nearly two decades, JourneyLite Physicians and JourneyLite Surgery Center have been at the forefront of surgical and medical weight management. With offices in Indianapolis, Columbus, Dayton, and Northern Kentucky—and our outpatient surgery center in the Cincinnati area—we’ve built a system designed from the ground up for weight-loss patients.

As we reflect on almost 20 years of minimally invasive bariatric surgery, our history is defined by innovation, rigorous safety standards, and a deep commitment to transforming our patients’ lives.

## JourneyLite Has a “History of Firsts,” Pioneering in Bariatric Care

Since our founding in 2007 by Medical Director Dr. Trace Curry, a board-certified general surgeon and Cincinnati native, JourneyLite has consistently led the way in the United States in bariatric innovation. Dr. Curry and the JourneyLite team have performed over 6,000  weight-loss procedures, driving advancements in outpatient care and non-surgical therapies.

Our pursuit of the safest, most effective treatments has resulted in an impressive list of “firsts” in the field of weight loss surgery:

-   2008: Dr. Curry placed the first Realize-C gastric band in Ohio (2nd in the United States).
-   2010: JourneyLite Surgery Center became the first ambulatory surgery center (ASC) to receive a “(https://link.springer.com/rwe/10.1007/978-3-030-60596-4_61), as well as the first ASC to receive “(https://www.bcbs.com/about-us/programs-initiatives/blue-distinction-specialty-care)” status.
-   2011: Dr. Curry performed the first (https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007435.htm) (VSG) in an ASC in the United States.
-   2013: JourneyLite pioneered the first same-day discharge gastric sleeve in the United States.
-   2015: Dr. Curry placed the first (https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/products/endoluminal-surgery-devices/orbera-intragastric-balloon-system.html) and ReShape gastric balloons in the Midwest.
-   2016: JourneyLite performed the first (https://asmbs.org/condition_procedures/single-anastomosis-duodeno-ileal-bypass-with-sleeve-gastrectomy/) procedure in an ASC setting in the Midwest.
-   2016: JourneyLite became the first ASC in the U.S. to receive (https://asmbs.org/about/mbsaqip/) accreditation as a Comprehensive Center.
-   2017: Dr. Curry published the (https://www.soard.org/article/S1550-7289(18)30094-7/abstract)
-   2019: JourneyLite was the first ASC to receive the (https://myoptum.phs.com/brs) designation.
-   2020: Dr. Curry performed the first Obalon gastric balloon placements in Ohio.
-   2022: Dr. Curry placed the first (https://www.spatzmedical.com/) in Ohio.
-   2024: Dr. Curry performed the first two commercial (https://www.allurion.com/en) placements in the United States.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about JourneyLite’s history, experience, and what makes our program different? Here are answers to common questions.

How long has JourneyLite been helping patients with surgical weight loss?

JourneyLite has spent nearly two decades focused on bariatric and metabolic care. Over that time, we’ve helped thousands of patients across Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana take lasting steps toward better health through surgery and long-term support.

What bariatric procedures does JourneyLite perform?

Our surgical team offers leading bariatric procedures including gastric sleeve (VSG), gastric bypass (Roux-en-Y), and SADI/SIPS. We also provide revisional surgery options when appropriate and comprehensive medical weight loss support.

Does JourneyLite offer gastric balloon?

Yes, JourneyLite is one of the most experienced gastric balloon centers in the country.

What does it mean to be an accredited bariatric surgery center?

Accreditation signals that a facility meets rigorous standards for safety, outcomes tracking, and quality improvement. JourneyLite Surgery Center has earned recognition for high standards in outpatient bariatric care and maintains a culture of continuous quality and patient safety.

Is outpatient bariatric surgery safe?

For appropriately selected patients, outpatient bariatric surgery can be very safe—especially in a dedicated facility designed specifically for these procedures. Your eligibility depends on your medical history, risk factors, and surgeon evaluation.

What makes JourneyLite different from a hospital-based bariatric program?

JourneyLite is built around a focused bariatric workflow—specialized staff, efficient scheduling, and long-term follow-up. Many patients appreciate the dedicated outpatient environment, consistent nutrition coaching, and frequent touchpoints that help support lasting results.

Do you offer support after surgery?

Yes. Long-term success is about more than the operation. JourneyLite provides structured follow-up, dietitian guidance, education, and ongoing check-ins to help patients stay on track and address plateaus or weight regain if it occurs.

Do you offer virtual visits or multiple office locations?

Yes. JourneyLite has multiple office locations across the region and offers virtual visits when appropriate, making it easier for patients to stay connected with their care team. You can view locations here: (https://journeylite.com/map-schedule-and-info/).

How do I schedule a consultation?

The first step is a consultation so we can review your goals, medical history, and the best options for you. Request an appointment here: (https://journeylite.com/appointment-request/).
