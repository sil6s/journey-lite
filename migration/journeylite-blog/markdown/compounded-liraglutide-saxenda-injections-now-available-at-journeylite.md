# Why Compounded Liraglutide?

Following recent legal restrictions on compounded **semaglutide** and **tirzepatide**, patients have been seeking effective alternatives for weight loss. Now that the **patent on liraglutide has expired**, we are able to offer this proven (https://journeylite.com/bariatric-surgery-vs-glp-1s-which-is-better/) at a fraction of the brand-name cost.

Compounded liraglutide (Saxenda) works by mimicking a natural hormone that targets areas of the brain involved in appetite regulation. It helps reduce hunger, improve satiety, and promote sustainable weight loss.

### Affordable, Simple Weight Loss Injections

One of the key advantages of compounded liraglutide is its **simple daily dosing regimen**. Patients gradually increase their dose over the first 4 weeks, reaching **maintenance dosing by week 5**.

-   **First month**: Just **$198** (for medication)

-   **Ongoing monthly cost**: Only **$298** (for medication)

There are **no insurance requirements**, **no hidden fees**, and **no long waits** to get started.

### Who Can Benefit from Compounded Liraglutide?

Liraglutide injections may be a great solution for patients who:

-   Have had a **bariatric surgery** in the past and need support to get back on track.

-   Are **post-op but have not yet reached their weight loss goal**.

-   Have **not had surgery** but continue to struggle with weight despite diet and exercise.
