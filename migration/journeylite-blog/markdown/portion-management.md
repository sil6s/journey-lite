## Body
✨ Portion power without the “diet” vibes ✨
Want to eat less without feeling restricted? Try these two brain-friendly tricks:

🍽️ **Smaller plate, smaller fork.** Your eyes see a full plate, but you’re actually serving less—easy win!
🔪 **Pre-cut your food.** Slice that chicken breast (or apple, steak, etc.) before you sit down. Smaller bites = slower eating = quicker fullness.

Tiny tweaks → big results.
Give these a shot today and keep your portions (and goals) on track. You’ve got this! 💪
