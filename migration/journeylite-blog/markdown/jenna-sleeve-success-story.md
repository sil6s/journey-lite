## Body
Jenna has struggled with weight most of her life.  Diets never worked, and no matter how much she worked out the scale never seemed to move.  That’s why she chose to have a sleeve gastrectomy with Metabolic Weight Loss Centers and Dr. Trace Curry.

She initially contacted our office in 2014 to try a non-surgical option.  “I lost weight with medications, but every time I went off the weight came back and then some” Jenna says.  Finally after reaching a high weight of nearly 225 lbs in 2015, she decided to make a life change.  She contacted our office to schedule an appointment for a surgical consultation and watched a informative video online to educate herself on all her options.  “I liked that I didn’t have to be herded into a room for a live seminar with a bunch of other people I didn’t know and that I could learn what I needed to know right from my home computer” says Jenna.  “I chose the sleeve gastrectomy for me as I felt it was the best operation to achieve my goals and help change my life.”

“From start to finish, Dr. Curry’s team was thorough and informative and took great care of me.”

She is now down almost 100 lbs and looking forward to her  new, healthier life!
