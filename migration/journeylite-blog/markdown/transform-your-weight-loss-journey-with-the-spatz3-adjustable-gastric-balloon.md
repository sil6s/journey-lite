## Spatz3 Adjustable Gastric Balloon at JourneyLite

Are you struggling to achieve your weight loss goals despite trying various diets and exercise routines? Not interested in surgery or a lifetime of expensive injectable weight loss meds with lots of side effects? JourneyLite and the (https://www.spatzmedical.com/) Adjustable Gastric Balloon might be the solution you’ve been looking for!

### **What is the Spatz3 Adjustable Gastric Balloon?**

The Spatz3 is the world’s first and only adjustable gastric balloon. This innovative device is designed to help you lose weight by occupying space in your stomach, making you feel fuller faster and reducing your overall food intake1. What sets the Spatz3 apart from other gastric balloons is its unique adjustability feature.

#### **How Does the Spatz3 Work?**

The Spatz3 gastric balloon is inserted through a quick, non-surgical outpatient procedure at (https://journeylite.com/map-schedule-and-info/) that takes about 15 minutes. There are no incisions or hospitalization required, and you can walk out the door within the hour2. Once in place, the balloon can be adjusted in volume to ensure maximum comfort and effectiveness.

##### **Benefits of the Spatz3 Adjustable Gastric Balloon**

1.  1.  **Customizable Fit**: The Spatz3 balloon can be adjusted both up and down to meet your individual needs, helping you avoid weight loss plateaus and achieve sustained results.
    2.  **High Success Rate**: With a clinically proven track record, the Spatz3 balloon boasts the highest weight loss and success rates in the industry.
    3.  **Comfort and Safety**: The adjustability feature allows for a reduction in balloon size if discomfort occurs, ensuring a more comfortable experience and reducing the need for early removal.
    4.  **Long-Term Weight Maintenance**: The Spatz3 balloon can stay in place for up 9 months, giving you ample time to develop healthy eating habits and maintain your weight loss.

#### **Real Success Stories!**

Many individuals have successfully transformed their lives with the Spatz3 Adjustable Gastric Balloon. For example, Julia lost over 110 pounds in just 12 months, while David completely transformed his health and wellness. These stories highlight the potential of the Spatz3 to help you achieve your weight loss goals.
