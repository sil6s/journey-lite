## Body
The ReShape Duo gastric balloon is a unique device that helps patients lose weight without surgery! Watch this brief infosession narrated by Dr. Trace Curry, a leading gastric balloon expert, to learn more about the procedure. The ReShape gastric balloon is placed while the patient is under light sedation and requires no surgery. The procedure takes only 20 minutes, and most patients can return to work in a few days. With some dedication to a healthy diet and regular exercise, patients can typically expect to lose 25-50 pounds. Our 12-month program is designed to help you lose the weight and develop the habits to keep it off. Registered dietitians will be involved in your care along the whole way, and every patient gets a free FitBit and a wireless scale that feeds data to the high-tech ReShape Patient Portal to help you stay on track. Dr. Trace Curry and his team are among the most experienced gastric balloon physicians in the United States and have been co-authors on numerous publications. Choose our team and start losing now!
