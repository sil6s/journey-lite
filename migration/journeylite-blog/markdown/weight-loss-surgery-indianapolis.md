**About JourneyLite Surgery Center**

JourneyLite Physicians & JourneyLite Surgery Center

JourneyLite Surgery Center was founded by Dr. Trace Curry in 2007, and is located in Cincinnati, Ohio. The center is dedicated to the care of weight loss surgery, plastics, spine, and pain management patients. JourneyLite is known for many “firsts” in the mid-west, such as the first outpatient same-day gastric sleeve surgery. Dr. Curry and JourneyLite published one of the largest series on the safety and efficacy of same-day discharge gastric sleeve procedures, showing equal results to patients who were kept for 1-2 days in the hospital afterwards.

JourneyLite is a MBSAQIP-accredited bariatric surgery center of excellence by the American College of Surgeons and also carries several other quality designations such as:
