**“Now, I get to be _me_—confident, healthy, and free.”**
_– Alexa, Gastric Sleeve Patient, April 2022_

As a teenager, I knew my weight was holding me back—not just physically, but emotionally. At 298 pounds, I struggled to feel confident in who I was. It affected every part of my life.

Since having gastric sleeve surgery in April 2022, everything has changed. I’m now 138 pounds, off multiple medications, and—most importantly—I feel like the best version of myself. The procedure didn’t just change my body—it gave me the confidence and freedom to truly _be me_.

JourneyLite made the entire process seamless. From start to finish, their team answered every question and supported me at every step. Their guidance gave me the clarity and confidence I needed to succeed.

If you’re ready to take your life back, I can tell you firsthand—it’s worth it.
