## Body
Portion control doesn’t have to feel restrictive – sometimes it’s as simple as grabbing your measuring cups and spoons. Using them to dish out meals and snacks takes the guesswork out of serving sizes and helps you stay consistent without relying on willpower alone. Over time, you’ll start to recognize what true portions look like, making mindful eating feel more natural. This small habit can make a big difference for anyone working toward weight loss, maintaining bariatric goals, or just wanting to feel more in control of their choices. Simple tools, big impact.
