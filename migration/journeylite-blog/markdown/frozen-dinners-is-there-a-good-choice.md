There are a few choices between Smart Ones and Lean Cuisine that are a good choice for a modified carbohydrate diet.

The general recommendation is that frozen entrée choices should stay under 20 net carbs per meal.

**Weight Watchers – Smart Ones**

**Breakfast Entrée Choices:**

-   Ham and Cheese Scramble (11 net carbs and 21 gm protein)
-   Cheesy Scramble with Hashbrowns (15 net carbs and 15 gm protein)

**Smart Anytime Choices:**

-   Chicken Slider (17 net carbs and 9 gm protein)
-   Mini Cheeseburger (17 net carbs and 10 gm protein)
-   Spicy Chicken Slider (17 net carbs and 8 gm protein)

**Entrée Meals:**

-   Chicken Santa Fe (7 net carbs and 20 gm protein)
-   Creamy Parmesan Chicken (20 net carbs and 18 gm protein)
-   Home Style Beef Pot Roast (15 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Meatloaf (with mashed potatoes) (19 net carbs and 21 gm protein)
-   Roast Beef (with mashed potatoes) (16 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Salisbury Steak (17 net carbs and 18 gm protein)
-   Slow Roasted Turkey Breast (16 net carbs and 17 gm protein)

**Desserts:**

-   No appropriate choices.

**Lean Cuisine**

-   Beef Pot Roast (19 net carbs and 13 gm protein)
-   Herb Roasted Chicken (15 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Roasted Garlic Chicken (10 net carbs and 17 gm protein)
-   Roasted Turkey and Vegetables (14 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Steak Tips Portabella (12 net carbs and 15 gm protein)

**Healthy Choice Meals**

**Baked Entrées:** 

-   Roasted Chicken and potatoes (18 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Slow Roasted Turkey Bake (15 net carbs and 13 gm protein)

**Steaming Entrées:**

-   Roasted Beef Merlot (16 net carbs and 17 gm protein)

**Select Entrées:**

-   Salisbury Steak (14 net carbs and 13 gm protein)

**Atkins Frozen Dinners**

**Breakfast Choices:**

-   Farmhouse-Style Sausage Scramble (5 net carbs and 22 gm protein)
-   Tex Mex Scrambler (5 net carbs and 22 gm protein)

**Entrée Choices:**

-   Beef Merlot (6 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Chicken and Broccoli Alfredo (5 net carbs and 26 gm protein)
-   Chicken Marsala (7 net carbs and 23 gm protein)
-   Chili Con Carne (4 net carbs and 24 gm protein)
-   Crustless Chicken Pot Pie (5 net carbs and 22 gm protein)
-   Italian Sausage Primavera (5 net carbs and 16 gm protein)
-   Italian-Style Pasta Bake (7 net carbs and 33 gm protein)
-   Meatloaf with Gravy (7 net carbs and 23 gm protein)
-   Roast Turkey with Gravy (6 net carbs and 23 gm protein)
-   Sesame Chicken Stir-Fry (7 net carbs and 26 gm protein)
