Justice

**“I finally learned how to build a healthier life—not just lose weight.”**

My name is **Justice**, and I had **gastric sleeve surgery with Dr. James Augusta at JourneyLite in April 2025**.

My highest adult weight was **297 pounds**, and I’m now down to **187 pounds**.

I first realized my weight was becoming a problem when I was a teenager, but for a long time I didn’t fully understand just how much it was affecting every part of my life. Looking back now, losing weight has improved both my **physical health and my mental health** in ways I never imagined.

My **self-esteem and confidence** have grown so much. I feel more comfortable in my body, and I’m able to show up more fully in everyday life. On top of that, many of my obesity-related health issues have improved — including **diabetes and PCOS symptoms** — and I have so much more **energy and mobility** now.

### How surgery changed my relationship with food

Having gastric sleeve surgery completely changed the way I think about food. It helped me learn how to:

-   control my portions

-   make healthier choices

-   fuel my body with intention

-   move my body in ways that are realistic and sustainable


That has been the biggest difference for me. This journey hasn’t just been about weight loss — it has been about learning how to live a healthier life in a way I can actually maintain.

### Why JourneyLite made such a big difference

The support from the JourneyLite team was a huge part of my success. Their guidance, education, and consistent encouragement helped me feel informed, prepared, and supported through every stage of the process.

Knowing I had a knowledgeable and compassionate team to rely on gave me confidence, helped me stay accountable, and made it easier to navigate both the physical and emotional parts of surgery and recovery.

### Why I chose surgery after trying GLP-1 medications

I had tried GLP-1 medications before surgery, but I ultimately chose surgery because I wanted something that would help me truly learn how to:

-   make healthy choices

-   fuel my body properly

-   control my portions

-   create a healthier, more sustainable lifestyle


For me, surgery became the tool that helped me finally build those habits.

### My biggest victory

One of the biggest changes in my life now is simple, but it means everything:

**I can go, go, go all day long without feeling out of breath or exhausted by everyday tasks.**

That kind of freedom is something I don’t take for granted.

I’m so thankful for Dr. Augusta and the JourneyLite team for helping me change my life.
