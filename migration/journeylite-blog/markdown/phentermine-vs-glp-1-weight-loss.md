## **A Conversation About Phentermine vs. GLP-1’s With Dr. Trace Curry**

Dr. Trace CurryMedical Director

Hi, I’m Dr. Trace Curry with JourneyLite Physicians.

If you’ve looked into weight-loss medications lately, you’ve probably heard a lot about **GLP-1 medications** — drugs like Wegovy®, Zepbound®, and Ozempic®. They’ve helped many people lose significant weight.

But one question I get all the time is:
**“If GLP-1s are so effective, why do doctors still prescribe phentermine?”**

That’s a great question — and the answer might surprise you. Let’s talk about **why phentermine is still one of the most useful tools** in a modern, medically supervised weight-loss plan.

## **GLP-1 Medications: Impressive but Imperfect**

GLP-1s work by mimicking a hormone that helps you feel full sooner and stay full longer. They slow digestion and reduce appetite, which can result in steady, impressive weight loss — often **10–15% of total body weight** in a year.

That said, there are a few downsides:

-   💰 **High cost:** GLP-1 drugs can run over $1,000 per month if not covered by insurance.
-   ⏱️ **Slow ramp-up:** Doses increase gradually over several months to minimize side effects.
-   🤢 **GI symptoms:** Nausea, bloating, constipation, or fatigue are common.
-   🔁 **Regain risk:** Once you stop, appetite usually returns — and so does the weight.

GLP-1s can be life-changing for some patients, especially those with **type 2 diabetes or severe obesity**. But for others, there’s a simpler, more affordable path.

## **Phentermine: A Proven, Affordable Starting Point**

Phentermine (also known as Adipex) has been around for decades — and it remains one of the **most effective appetite suppressants** available when prescribed and monitored correctly.

Here’s why I still use it often in my practice at JourneyLite:

### **1\. It works quickly**

Phentermine starts curbing appetite within hours. For many patients, that early success provides a huge psychological boost and keeps motivation high.

### **2\. It’s affordable**

Generic phentermine often costs **under $30 a month** — a fraction of the cost of weekly GLP-1 injections. For patients without insurance coverage, that’s a major advantage.

### **3\. It’s easy to take**

It’s a simple **once-a-day pill** — no injections, no refrigeration, and no complicated schedules.

### **4\. It’s well-understood and time-tested**

Because phentermine has been used safely for decades, we know how to screen for side effects and monitor your progress closely. Most patients tolerate it well when properly selected.

### **5\. It can jump-start lifestyle change**

At JourneyLite, we use phentermine as part of a comprehensive plan that includes nutrition, activity, and behavioral support. Patients often lose **5–10% of body weight** in the first few months — a great foundation for long-term success.

## **GLP-1s vs Phentermine: Which Fits You Best?**

**Phentermine**
Cost: 💲 Low
Form: Daily pill
Results: Rapid
Side Effects: Mild jitters, dry mouth
Coverage: Usually affordable even without
Best For: Kickstarting weight loss, shorter-term goals

**GLP-1 Meds (Wegovy®, Zepbound®)**
Cost: 💰 High (brand name only)
Form: Weekly injection
Results: Gradual
Side Effects: Nausea, constipation, fatigue, GERD
Coverage: Often limited or denied
Best For: Long-term management, metabolic disease

### **My Recommendation as a Bariatric Specialist**

When I meet with a new patient, we always look at the **whole picture** — health conditions, weight-loss goals, cost, and lifestyle.

Here’s how I typically decide:

-   **Short-term weight loss or budget concerns?** → Phentermine is usually my first choice.
-   **Cardiovascular disease or diabetes?** → GLP-1s may offer more metabolic benefit.
-   **Combination approach needed?** → Sometimes we use phentermine _before or after_ GLP-1 therapy to help transition and maintain results.

Remember: neither drug is a “magic pill.” The best outcomes come from **medication plus lifestyle change** — and that’s exactly what we specialize in at JourneyLite.  Even better outcomes are seen with surgical procedures like the (https://journeylite.com/surgical-weight-loss/gastric-sleeve-vsg/), which yield up to [5x the weight loss than even the most powerful GLP-1](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1086809?).

## **When Phentermine Might Not Be Right for You**

Phentermine isn’t ideal if you have:

-   Uncontrolled high blood pressure
-   Heart rhythm issues
-   Anxiety or insomnia
-   A history of stimulant misuse
-   Patients who are already on stimulants for ADHD

## **The Bottom Line**

Phentermine is **not old-fashioned** — it’s a **proven, accessible, and effective option** when prescribed safely and monitored by an experienced medical team.

GLP-1 drugs like Wegovy® or Zepbound® have their place, but phentermine remains a practical and empowering tool — especially for motivated patients ready to take control of their weight.
