# Our patients rate us highly!

Sometimes when you see a bunch of 5 star reviews you think to yourself “are these real or fake?”  Chances are, if there are only 5 star reviews and no 1 star reviews they probably are!  At JourneyLite, we pride ourselves in our top-notch patient care and regularly receive glowing, old-fashioned hand-written patient satisfaction surveys from our patients.  We have de-identified these and posted our Q4 2019 surveys just to show how our patients feel about our experienced and caring team.
