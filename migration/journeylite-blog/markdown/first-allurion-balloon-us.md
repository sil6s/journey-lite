## What is the Allurion Gastric Balloon?

The Allurion Balloon is the world’s first and only swallowable gastric balloon that does not typically require an endoscopy for insertion or removal. Unlike traditional gastric balloons that require a surgical or endoscopic procedure for placement and removal, the Allurion system is designed for simplicity and comfort.

**Swallowable “Smart” Capsule:** The patient swallows a small capsule containing the deflated balloon under the guidance of our clinical team and Dr. Curry.
**No Anesthesia:** Because there is no endoscopy required for routine cases, patients avoid the risks and downtime associated with sedation.
**Natural Clearance:** After approximately 16 weeks, the balloon automatically deflates and passes naturally through the digestive system.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Allurion Balloon

### Is anesthesia really not required for the Allurion placement?

Correct. Because the Allurion gastric balloon is a "smart capsule" that is swallowed under the guidance of Dr. Curry and our clinical team, there is no need for endoscopy, anesthesia, or sedation. You are fully awake and comfortable during the approximately 15-minute office visit.

### What does it feel like to swallow the capsule?

Most patients swallow the small capsule easily with a glass of water, much like a large vitamin. If you have any difficulty, Dr. Curry uses a very thin guiding stylet to assist with the placement, but no active sedation is required. Patients generally report that the process is quick and much simpler than expected.

### How much weight will I lose on the Allurion program?

Clinical studies show that, on average, patients lose between 10% and 15% of their total body weight during the 16-week balloon placement. These results are sustained long-term by participating in the JourneyLite support program, which includes nutritional coaching and digital tracking tools.

### Does the Allurion balloon require a doctor visit for removal?

No, this is the main advantage of the "flush" balloon technology. After approximately 16 weeks, a pre-set release valve opens, allowing the balloon to automatically deflate. It then passes naturally and safely through your digestive system, with no removal procedure required.

### Who is a good candidate for this non-surgical balloon?

The Allurion program is generally designed for adults with a Body Mass Index (BMI) of 27 or greater who have been unable to achieve weight loss through diet and exercise alone. It is a powerful option for those who are not candidates for—or prefer to avoid—surgery. A free consultation at JourneyLite will confirm if it is right for you.
