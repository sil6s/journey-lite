**Alicia**

**“I have my confidence—and my life—back.”**
_– Alicia, Gastric Sleeve Patient, July 2024_

After serving briefly in the U.S. Air Force, an injury left me unable to stay active. Add to that years of raising kids, the heartbreak of miscarriages, and a sedentary career as an over-the-road truck driver, and my weight quickly spiraled out of control. At my highest, I was 287 pounds, battling daily joint pain, back issues, and the emotional toll of feeling stuck in a body that didn’t reflect who I was inside.

I had tried everything—diet pills, low-carb, keto—but I couldn’t get below 250. It was frustrating and defeating. In early 2024, I found JourneyLite and met with Dr. Augusta. That was the turning point. On July 25, I had gastric sleeve surgery, and it changed everything.

Today, I weigh 140 pounds. I’ve gone from a size 22 to a size 8, and from a 2XL to a small. I can cross my legs, sit comfortably on the floor, and walk 8+ miles a day without pain. The surgery didn’t just give me weight loss—it gave me a second chance.

The support from JourneyLite has been phenomenal. Whether it was the office staff, surgical team, or dietitians, they were always available—just a message or text away. They prepared me for the ups and downs, and that honest, caring guidance helped me succeed.

If you’re considering weight loss surgery or medical options, I can’t recommend JourneyLite enough. They helped me reclaim my health, my confidence, and the joy I’d been missing for years.
