## Body
!(/legacy-blog/do-i-have-coverage-for-saxenda__Saxenda2.jpg.webp.webp)Saxenda can be a very effective weight loss tool, however it is important to know your coverage and out-of-pocket expenses in order for us to determine whether it is a viable option for you.

Fortunately Vivus, the manufacturer or Saxenda, provides a help line to assist patients with figuring this out.

Before you call the help line make sure you have:

-   Your insurance card
-   Your pharmacy prescription card (if applicable)
-   A (https://www.novocare.com/saxenda/savings-card.html)

The coverage help line number is 1-888-809-3942.  Please ask the case manager the following questions:

-   Do I have coverage? (Yes/No)
-   If yes, what is my out-of-pocket cost?
-   Is a prior authorization required? (Yes/No)

Make sure you write this all down and bring it to your appointment, as we cannot prescribe Saxenda until we can review this information!
