## Body
Recently, the FDA approved Belviq for the chronic treatment of obesity.  Belviq is the only drug approved for long-term use, and does not have the restrictions that we see with the use of Adipex (phentermine). There are still restrictions on BMI however:  Your BMI has to be either above 30, or between 27-30 with a weight related medical issue.  It is not approved for use with BMI less than 27.
