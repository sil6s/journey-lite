**BREADLESS STUFFING**

We made this for support group years ago and it was a big hit!  It’s a great dish to have for the holidays. –       1 lb pork sausage (can use turkey sausage) –       ¼ cup celery, chopped –       ¼ cup onion, chopped –       2 eggs –       1 head cauliflower, coarsely chopped (about 2 cups) –       1 Tbsp fresh parsley (1 tsp dried) –       3 Tbsp fresh sage (1 ½ tsp dried) –       3 Tbsp fresh thyme (1 ½ tsp dried) –       1 Tbsp garlic –       1/8 tsp salt –       1/8 tsp pepper **Directions:** Preheat oven to 350˚F. Cook sausage in skillet until browned, then add celery and onion and cook until softened.  Drain Fat.  Beat eggs in a bowl then add to sausage mixture.  Add all remaining ingredients.  Put mixture into an 8 x 8 baking dish and Bake for approximately 30 minutes. **Yields:**  10 servings (1/2 cup = 1 serving) **Nutrition Information:**
