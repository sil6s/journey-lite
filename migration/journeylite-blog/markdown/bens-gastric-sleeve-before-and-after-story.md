**“From 472 lbs to 254—and a life I can fully participate in.”**

I’m Ben, and in November 2020 I had a gastric sleeve with Dr. Trace Curry at JourneyLite. I’d struggled with my weight since young adulthood. It wasn’t just the physical strain—everyday tasks felt harder than they should, social situations chipped away at my confidence, and I worried constantly about my long-term health. It was exhausting.

Choosing surgery wasn’t easy, but it was the turning point I needed. The sleeve quieted the relentless hunger and gave me space to build habits that actually feel sustainable. Today I’m down from 472 lbs to 254, and I feel more energetic, more confident, and more in control of my life than I have in years.

What made the biggest difference was the support at JourneyLite. Their team checked in, answered every question, and coached me through nutrition and routines. I never felt alone—just consistently guided and encouraged. That accountability kept me focused through the first year and beyond.

The wins show up in small moments that add up to a big life: tying my shoes without a struggle, climbing stairs without stopping, walking long distances without running out of breath, and showing up to social events without second-guessing myself. I can simply move through my day with comfort—and that feels incredible.
