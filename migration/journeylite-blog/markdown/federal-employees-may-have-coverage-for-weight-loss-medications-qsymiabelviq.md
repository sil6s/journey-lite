## Body
According to some new information released by federal agencies, federal employees cannot be denied coverage for weight loss medications such as Qsymia and Belviq. This is a major step in a positive direction towards access to treatment for the disease of obesity! We have been utilizing weight loss medications such as phentermine (Adipex), tenuate, Qsymia, and Belviq for years and have seen excellent results, both as a primary method of achieving weight loss and as a combination therapy to augment the success of surgical weight loss patients.  Patients who have had sleeve gastrectomy or gastric band/Lap Band can utilize these medications to get a jump start and get back on track!
