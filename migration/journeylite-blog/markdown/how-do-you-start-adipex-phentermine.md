## Step 1: Schedule a Medical Evaluation

Before starting Adipex, you must have a **medical evaluation** with a licensed provider. This visit typically includes:

-   Review of medical history and medications

-   Weight, BMI, and vital signs

-   Discussion of prior weight loss attempts

-   Screening for conditions that may prevent safe use

Not everyone is a candidate for phentermine, and patient safety always comes first.

* * *

## Step 2: Determine Eligibility

You may qualify for Adipex if you:

-   Have a BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with weight-related conditions

-   Do not have contraindications such as uncontrolled hypertension or certain heart conditions

-   Are able to participate in regular follow-up visits

Eligibility is determined on an individual basis.

* * *

## Step 3: Begin Treatment With Monitoring

If approved, your provider will:

-   Prescribe the appropriate dose

-   Review potential side effects

-   Set expectations for weight loss and lifestyle changes

-   Schedule follow-up visits to monitor progress and safety

Regular follow-up is required to continue treatment.

* * *

## Step 4: Combine Medication With Lifestyle Changes

Phentermine works best when paired with:

-   A structured nutrition plan

-   Portion control and protein-focused meals

-   Physical activity appropriate for your ability

-   Ongoing accountability and support

Medication alone is not a long-term solution — sustainable habits are essential.
