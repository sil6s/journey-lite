## Outpatient General Surgery Services

Many common general surgery procedures can be performed on an outpatient basis, meaning patients typically go home the same day. JourneyLite combines surgical experience with a streamlined practice model to help patients move from consultation to treatment efficiently.

### Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Laparoscopic cholecystectomy is a minimally invasive procedure to remove the gallbladder. It is commonly performed for patients with symptomatic gallstones, gallbladder attacks, biliary colic, or certain other gallbladder problems.

-   Small-incision laparoscopic approach
-   Often performed as an outpatient procedure
-   Commonly indicated for recurrent gallbladder pain or gallstones

### Hernia Repair

Hernias occur when tissue pushes through a weakened area of the abdominal wall. JourneyLite evaluates and treats select hernias, including common abdominal wall hernias.

-   Umbilical hernias
-   Ventral hernias
-   Incisional hernias
-   Other select abdominal wall hernias

### Upper Endoscopy (EGD)

Upper endoscopy allows the surgeon to evaluate the esophagus, stomach, and upper small intestine using a flexible camera. This can be helpful for patients with reflux symptoms, abdominal pain, swallowing problems, or bariatric surgery-related concerns.

-   Evaluation of reflux or heartburn
-   Assessment of stomach or upper GI symptoms
-   Pre-operative or post-operative bariatric evaluation when appropriate

### Lipoma & Skin Lesion Excision

JourneyLite offers removal of select lipomas and skin lesions when surgical excision is appropriate. These procedures are often performed for lesions that are growing, uncomfortable, irritated, or concerning in appearance.

-   Lipoma removal
-   Removal of select cysts or benign-appearing lesions
-   Excision of irritated or bothersome skin lesions

Not sure whether your condition is appropriate for JourneyLite? Our team can review your information and help determine whether a consultation with one of our surgeons is the right next step.

## Conditions We Commonly Evaluate

Patients may be referred to JourneyLite or request an appointment for a variety of general surgery concerns. Common reasons for evaluation include:

### Gallbladder Symptoms

-   Right upper abdominal pain
-   Pain after fatty meals
-   Nausea or vomiting related to gallbladder attacks
-   Known gallstones on imaging

### Hernia Symptoms

-   A bulge in the abdomen, groin or belly button area
-   Discomfort with lifting, coughing, or straining
-   Prior incision with a new bulge
-   Increasing size or symptoms over time

### Upper GI Symptoms

-   Reflux or heartburn
-   Difficulty swallowing
-   Upper abdominal discomfort
-   Need for pre-operative or post-operative endoscopic evaluation

### Skin or Soft Tissue Concerns

-   Lipomas
-   Skin lesions that rub, bleed, or become irritated
-   Subcutaneous masses
-   Lesions that need surgical evaluation

## General Surgery FAQ

Does JourneyLite perform gallbladder removal?

Yes. JourneyLite performs laparoscopic cholecystectomy (gallbladder removal) for appropriate patients with gallstones or gallbladder-related symptoms.

What types of hernias does JourneyLite treat?

JourneyLite evaluates and treats select abdominal wall hernias, including umbilical, ventral, and incisional hernias. The best approach depends on the size, location, symptoms, and patient-specific factors.

Does JourneyLite offer endoscopy?

Yes. Upper endoscopy (EGD) may be used to evaluate reflux, swallowing problems, abdominal symptoms, or bariatric surgery-related concerns when clinically appropriate.

Can JourneyLite remove lipomas or skin lesions?

JourneyLite offers excision of select lipomas and skin lesions. A consultation can help determine whether removal is appropriate and whether the procedure can be performed in the office or surgical setting.

Are these procedures outpatient?

Many of the procedures performed by JourneyLite are outpatient procedures, meaning patients often return home the same day. The exact plan depends on the procedure, medical history, and surgeon recommendation.

Do you offer self-pay (cash pay) options for general surgery?

Yes. JourneyLite offers transparent self-pay options for select outpatient general surgery procedures, and pricing is often less than half of hospital-based surgery. We can provide a clear estimate after reviewing your symptoms and prior testing.

How do I schedule a general surgery consultation?

You can request an appointment online using JourneyLite’s appointment request form. Our team will help determine the appropriate next step based on your symptoms, history, and surgical needs.
