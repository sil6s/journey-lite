## Body
Just 2 days with my FitBit so far but this thing is really great! It’s so easy to add foods you eat through the day, and if you set up a food plan it tells you exactly how many calories you can have vs. calories you burn. The sleep monitoring function is super cool as well. I really think this would be a big help to anyone trying to lose weight, or even just to maintain weight. Happy Losing, Dr. Curry
