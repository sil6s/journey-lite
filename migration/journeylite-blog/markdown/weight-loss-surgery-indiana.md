## Weight Loss Surgery for Indiana Patients

For patients pursuing surgery, JourneyLite provides comprehensive bariatric surgery options through our main office and surgery center in Cincinnati, approximately two hours from Indianapolis. We have patients who come to JourneyLite Surgery Center from all over Indiana, and patients can complete their initial evaluation and follow-up through JourneyLite’s Indianapolis-area clinic while receiving surgery from an experienced team at our (https://journeylite.com/the-center).

Our surgical weight loss options include:

### Gastric Sleeve

Also known as (https://journeylite.com/gastric-sleeve-surgery/), gastric sleeve reduces the size of the stomach to help you eat less, feel full sooner, and achieve meaningful long-term weight loss.

### Lap Band

(https://journeylite.com/lap-band-surgery/) involves placing an adjustable band around the upper part of the stomach to create a smaller pouch. This helps reduce food intake and can be adjusted over time based on your progress.

### Gastric Bypass

With (https://journeylite.com/gastric-bypass-surgery/), a small stomach pouch is created and part of the small intestine is bypassed. This helps patients lose weight by reducing intake and decreasing calorie absorption.

### SADI Surgery

(https://journeylite.com/sadi-surgery/) combines sleeve gastrectomy with an intestinal bypass to promote substantial weight loss and metabolic improvement for appropriate candidates.

### Gastric Band Revision

For patients who have had problems with prior Lap Band surgery or inadequate weight loss, (https://journeylite.com/gastric-band-revision/) may provide a better long-term solution.

### Gastric Sleeve Revision

If you have experienced weight regain, reflux, or other issues after sleeve gastrectomy, (https://journeylite.com/gastric-sleeve-revision-surgery/) may help address those concerns and improve results.

JourneyLite supports patients throughout the full process, from consultation and decision-making through surgery, recovery, and long-term follow-up.

The (https://journeylite.com/gastric-balloon-surgery/) is a temporary, non-surgical option that places a soft balloon in the stomach to help you feel full sooner and eat less. It can be a strong option for patients seeking meaningful weight loss without bariatric surgery.
