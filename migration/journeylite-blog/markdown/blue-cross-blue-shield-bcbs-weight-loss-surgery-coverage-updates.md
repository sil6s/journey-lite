## Does Blue Cross Blue Shield Insurance Cover Weight Loss Surgery?

**Most Blue Cross Blue Shield insurance programs cover at least some types of weight loss surgery**. Some options, such as vagus (or vagal) nerve-blocking devices, typically are not covered. Understand that some employers may opt for cheaper insurance plans that don’t include bariatric surgery. Be sure to check with your insurance provider for its specific requirements and restrictions.

## BCBS Bariatric Surgery Requirements 2024

**Many Blue Cross Blue Shield insurance programs have similar policies concerning weight loss surgery**. For bariatric surgery to be considered medically necessary, all the following criteria must be met:

### BMI

In most cases, a person with a BMI of 40 or more is considered eligible for weight loss surgery under BCBS regulations.

If a person has a BMI of 35 or greater and has one of the following conditions, BCBS will likely deem them eligible for bariatric surgery:

-   Diabetes mellitus
-   Hypertension
-   Cardiovascular disease
-   Life-threatening cardio-pulmonary conditions (such as severe obstructive sleep apnea or obesity-related cardiomyopathy)

A few BCBS policies will cover weight loss surgery if the person has a BMI of 30 or greater and type 2 diabetes

### Age

Most BCBS plans require the person to be at least 18, although some have provisions for adolescents.

### Pre- and Post-Operative Requirements

BMI and age aren’t the only requirements for eligibility under BCBS rules. Many BCBS policies require documentation of things like:

-   Previous participation in a weight loss program
-   Pre-operative mental health and medical assessments and clearances
-   Extensive preoperative education
-   A treatment plan addressing the patient’s pre- and post-operative needs

## Weight Loss Surgery Covered by Anthem Blue Cross

**Anthem Blue Cross, which applies to more than 9 million lives across several states, does not cover weight loss surgery for people with a BMI of less than 35**. However, for individuals who meet its requirements, it typically covers these types of weight loss surgery:

### Lap Band

During Lap Band surgery, we surround the upper stomach with a silicone band to create a smaller stomach pouch, which promotes weight loss because the person feels fuller more quickly.

### Gastric Sleeve

We remove about 80% of the patient’s stomach during gastric sleeve surgery to create a smaller, sleeve-shaped organ that holds less food. This aids in weight loss because the smaller stomach helps decrease hunger and food intake.

### Gastric Bypass

Gastric bypass surgery, also called Roux-en-Y, decreases stomach size and modifies the digestive tract to restrict food intake and nutrient absorption.

## BCBS Coverage Policies

While BCBS, one of the largest healthcare insurers in the United States, offers various policies that cover bariatric surgery, these can vary significantly between states and individual plans. Here’s how some plans differ:

### BCBS Ohio Weight Loss Surgery Coverage

Because BCBS Ohio, which has over 3 million members, is part of Anthem Blue Cross, it doesn’t cover bariatric surgery for people with a BMI under 35.

### Blue Cross Blue Shield Kentucky Bariatric Surgery Coverage

Like BCBS Ohio, Blue Cross Blue Shield Kentucky, which has nearly 2 million members, is operated by Anthem Blue Cross. People with a BMI under 35 aren’t covered for weight loss surgery under this plan.

### BCBS Indiana Weight Loss Surgery Coverage

BCBS Indiana, which covers over 4 million people, is also included in Anthem Blue Cross, so it doesn’t offer coverage for weight loss surgery for people with a BMI of less than 35.

### BCBS Michigan/Blue Care Network

BCBS Michigan/Blue Care Network, which covers nearly 5 million lives, has revised its policy on bariatric surgery to expand coverage for patients with a BMI of 30 to 35 with type 2 diabetes. To see the policy revisions from this carrier, click here: (https://www.bcbsm.com/amslibs/content/dam/public/mpr/mprsearch/pdf/76831.pdf)

### Premera Blue Cross

Premera Blue Cross, which covers nearly 2.5 million lives, has also revised its policy and expanded coverage for patients with a BMI of 30 to 35 with type 2 diabetes (although they do have some glycemic control restrictions, requiring a hemoglobin A1c level above 7). To see the policy revisions from this carrier, click here: (https://www.premera.com/hmo/medicalpolicies/7.01.516.pdf#search=bariatric)

We’re hopeful that other BCBS plans will follow the example set by BCBS Michigan/Blue Care Network and Premera.

To maximize your BCBS weight loss surgery coverage, be sure to follow all the regulations concerning preoperative weight loss attempts and medical and mental health assessments and clearances.

### At What BMI Will Insurance Cover Weight Loss Surgery?

Many BCBS plans will cover weight loss surgery for people with a BMI of 40 or greater. People with a BMI of 35 or greater may be eligible for coverage if they have a serious related condition.
