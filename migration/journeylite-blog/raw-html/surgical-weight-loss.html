Which Weight Loss Surgery Is Right for You?

There is no single “best” bariatric procedure for every patient. Gastric sleeve may be the right fit for one person, while gastric bypass, SADI/SIPS, or a revision may be a better choice for another.

During your consultation, JourneyLite reviews your BMI, weight history, medical conditions, reflux symptoms, prior surgeries, insurance or self-pay options, and long-term goals to help guide the decision.

Why Surgery Works Differently

Bariatric surgery is not just about restriction or “eating less.” Surgery can affect hunger, fullness, metabolism, blood sugar, and long-term weight regulation.

  • More durable weight loss for many patients
  • Improvement in obesity-related conditions
  • Structured follow-up and nutrition support
  • Options for primary and revision surgery

Most Popular

Gastric Sleeve / VSG

Gastric sleeve surgery reduces the stomach into a smaller sleeve-shaped pouch. It does not reroute the intestines and is one of the most commonly performed bariatric procedures.

  • Strong option for many first-time surgery patients
  • No intestinal bypass
  • Can produce significant, durable weight loss
Gastric Sleeve Details $10,000 Self-Pay Offer Before & After Results
Powerful Metabolic Option

Gastric Bypass

Gastric bypass combines a smaller stomach pouch with intestinal rerouting. It may be especially useful for selected patients with significant reflux, diabetes, or certain revision needs.

  • Strong metabolic effect
  • Often helpful for reflux-prone patients
  • Common revision option after Lap Band or sleeve
Gastric Bypass Details Pricing & Financing
Advanced Procedure

SADI / SIPS Surgery

SADI/SIPS combines sleeve gastrectomy with intestinal bypass to create a powerful weight-loss and metabolic procedure for selected patients.

  • May be considered for higher BMI patients
  • Can be used in selected revision settings
  • Requires long-term vitamin and nutrition follow-up
SADI/SIPS Details Pricing & Financing
Adjustable Option

Lap Band

Lap Band surgery uses an adjustable band around the upper stomach. It is less commonly chosen today than sleeve or bypass, but it may still be considered for certain patients.

  • Adjustable and reversible
  • No stomach removal
  • Requires ongoing band follow-up and adjustments
Lap Band Details Pricing & Financing
Second Chance

Revisional Bariatric Surgery

Some patients need revision surgery because of weight regain, reflux, band problems, inadequate weight loss, or complications from a prior bariatric procedure.

  • Band-to-sleeve or band-to-bypass options
  • Sleeve-to-bypass or sleeve-to-SADI in selected cases
  • Individualized based on anatomy and goals

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best weight loss surgery?
The best operation depends on your BMI, medical conditions, reflux history, prior surgeries, weight-loss goals, and long-term preferences. Gastric sleeve, gastric bypass, and SADI/SIPS each have different strengths.
Is gastric sleeve the most common procedure?
Gastric sleeve is one of the most commonly performed bariatric procedures and is often a strong option for first-time surgery patients, but it is not right for everyone.
When is gastric bypass a better choice?
Gastric bypass may be preferred in selected patients with significant reflux, certain metabolic conditions, or specific revision needs. A surgeon can help determine whether bypass is a better option than sleeve.
Who should consider SADI/SIPS?
SADI/SIPS may be considered for selected patients who need a more powerful weight-loss or metabolic procedure. It requires careful long-term nutrition and vitamin follow-up.
Does JourneyLite offer revision surgery?
Yes. JourneyLite evaluates patients for revisional bariatric surgery, including options after Lap Band, sleeve, bypass, or other prior procedures.
Can I start with a virtual visit?
Many patients can begin with a virtual consultation, especially those traveling from outside Cincinnati. The JourneyLite team can help determine what is appropriate for your situation.
